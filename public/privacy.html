Privacy notice

Last updated: September 24, 2026

This notice explains the information LinguistAI processes when you use its language lessons, exam practice, flashcards, tutor, study rooms, and language exchange features.

Information you provide

Your account provider supplies account identifiers and may supply your email, name, or profile image. Learning features can process notes, extracted text, lesson prompts, quiz answers, messages, and language-learning progress that you choose to enter or save.

Files and offline storage

PDF text extraction and image OCR run in your browser. The original file is not sent to LinguistAI's OCR server. If you ask the app to generate a lesson, quiz, or transcription from extracted text, the relevant text or audio is sent to the selected AI provider. Saved lectures and flashcards may be cached in your browser for offline access. Exam-portal progress is stored in account-scoped browser storage and, when available, synced to your LinguistAI account.

Service providers

LinguistAI uses Supabase for sign-in and database services. AI features send the relevant prompt or content to Groq or OpenAI. Speech playback can use Microsoft Edge's speech service or OpenAI text-to-speech. Each provider processes information under its own terms and privacy practices.

How information is used

Information is used to provide requested lessons, quizzes, speech, saved study material, progress tracking, room conversations, and language-exchange features. The app applies account-level database access policies to private learning records and conversations.

Your choices

You can avoid sending material to an AI provider by not using AI generation on that material. You can clear browser storage to remove local offline copies; this does not delete copies already saved to your account database. For account-data questions or deletion requests, contact the project maintainers.

Questions or requests?

Contact the LinguistAI maintainers through the project support page.
